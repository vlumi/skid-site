Need a hand, found a bug, or have an idea? Here's how to reach it.

## Report a bug or request a feature

Skid Jam is developed in the open. The best place for bugs and requests is
the issue tracker:

- [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/vlumi/skid/issues)

Please include your device and OS version, how many players, and the steps to
reproduce — it makes fixes much faster.

## Common questions

**How many players, and do I need more than one device?**
Two to four players on a single device — phone, iPad, or Mac. No second
device and no network needed; everyone shares the one screen.

**What's the difference between Casual and Pro?**
Casual is aim-to-drive — point where you want to go and the car drifts there.
Pro is a direct throttle/steer pad (with the same drift on tap). Each player
chooses their own in setup.

**Can I play on Mac, iPhone, and iPad?**
Yes — it's one free app across all three, from a single App Store listing.

**Where are my best laps and ghosts?**
On your device, and nowhere else. Your records and their replay ghosts are
stored locally.

**Contact vs. Ghost racing?**
Contact means cars collide and bump; Ghost lets everyone pass through each
other for pure, tangle-free speed. Set it per race.
