Couch multiplayer, one device

2–4 players share the screen — thumbs at the edges, the track clear in the middle. Sit side by side or across the table; each player's controls rotate to face them. No second device, no network, no fuss.

Drift, don't dab the brake

The Casual scheme is aim-to-drive: push where you want to go and the car flips into a drift, momentum carrying you round. Prefer direct control? Pro gives you throttle and steering — with the same drift on tap. Pick per player.

Tracks with a top deck

Ribbons of asphalt with kerbs, hazards, and two-layer circuits — ramps launch you onto a bridge that crosses right over the road below. Every corner is hand-tuned; every surface drawn procedurally, no image assets anywhere.

Chase the clock

Time trial pits you against your own best lap — a translucent ghost of your record run drives alongside, to out-brake and out-drift corner by corner.

Yours, on your devices

One free app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Best laps and ghosts stay on your device. No ads, no accounts, no tracking — ever.
